What the club is about

The old site says it simply: a DIY campus club at UW that wants to spark creativity and raise money for a great cause. This version keeps that message front and center.

Creative, welcoming, hands-on

We host low-pressure crafting sessions where anyone can try something new, learn a process, and leave with something tangible. The focus is on making, sharing, and having a good time while building a stronger club community.

How the club works

Members help design, make, and sell crafts. We also run events that make it easy to get involved, whether you want to craft, volunteer, donate, or just keep up with what we are building next.

FAQ

A short set of answers that covers the basics students usually want before joining.

Do I need to be artsy?

No. The club is for anyone who wants to make things, learn, and help out. We keep projects approachable.

Can I join if I only want to volunteer?

Yes. We always need help with setup, crafting, event promotion, sales, and cleanup.
