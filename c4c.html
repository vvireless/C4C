What the club does

Creative, welcoming, hands-on

We host low-pressure crafting sessions where anyone can try something new, learn a process, and leave with something tangible. The focus is on making, sharing, and having a good time while building a stronger club community.

How the club works

Members help design, make, and sell crafts. We also run events that make it easy to get involved, whether you want to craft, volunteer, donate, or just keep up with what we are building next.

FAQ

Do I need to be artsy?

All levels of experience are welcome! The club is for anyone who wants to make things, learn, and help out. We keep projects approachable.

How can I join if I want to volunteer?

Around the beginning of each term, we typically have listings for new positions. Check our social media or contact us for more information.

Where do donations go?

Each post on our instagram will show where the funds are going and how they are being used to support the cause.

How do I stay updated?

Instagram is our most active account to see photos, schedule changes, and quick reminders.
